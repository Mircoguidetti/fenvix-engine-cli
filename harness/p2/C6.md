# P2 C6: the engine's fixed cost per test file (founder decision R2 = A, 2026-11-27)

Judged on agentsys (Jest) and alakajam (Vitest, one process per test file), at 1 worker, on the branch head after the
release-1 7cbe7b27 merge (P3's alakajam fix: alakajam passes 217/217 under the engine). Plain serial for reference:
agentsys 12.9-13.3 s, alakajam 29-32 s; the engine at 1 worker: agentsys 17-21 s, alakajam 58-63 s.

## What the profiles show

- agentsys (`data/c6-before/agentsys`, main + Jest worker profiles): engine self 4.87 s of 22.7 s profiled; the largest
  engine function is `resolveDepth` (sites.js) at 1.7 s self.
- alakajam: Vitest kills its fork workers, so `--cpu-prof` writes no worker profile. With an inspector hook in the
  worker (a measurement aid in the scratchpad, not committed) on one file, `server/entry/highscore/highscore.service.spec.ts`:
  the engine takes 4.5-4.8 s against 2.3 s plain; Vitest's own breakdown is import 0.54 -> 2.06 s, tests 0.96 -> 1.40 s.
  Inclusive in the worker profile: 1.81 s of 4.35 s inside `byProjectOutside` (`src/outside.js`), mostly the
  repository's `@cspotcode/source-map-support` formatting stacks and reading map files. `DETJS_CHILD_PACE_MS=0` (P3's
  real-pace rule off) changes nothing (4.73 s against 4.67 s).

## Tried and reverted

1. The outside-files recorder reading the raw stack (e6b7359f, reverted in ffae5671 + 1a20e23b). `byProjectOutside()`
   formats `String(new Error().stack)` with 40 frames on every fs call made outside a test (every existsSync of module
   resolution at import). Building the same text from the raw call sites (its decision looks only at Node's module
   loader frames and detjs' own) made alakajam 61-63 s -> 43 s at 1 worker, agentsys, yalc and codex-multi-auth
   unchanged. Not identical on alakajam (`data/c6-ident/IDENTITY.txt`):
   - `files_read_outside_tests` 311 -> 149 entries: the 162 gone are reads of project `.ts` files made by
     source-map-support itself while the engine formatted its own stack (`@cspotcode/...` is scoped, so `INFRA` does not
     match it).
   - In 5 tests one `time` event's site moved from `server/core/forms.ts:255` (the `new Date()` line, correct) to
     `:238` (wrong): the site lookup (`resolveFormatted`) mapped that file correctly only after the import-time
     formatting had made source-map-support load its map.
   Its test `node/test/p2-c6-outside-stack.test.js` was deleted with it (new in e6b7359f, never released).
2. A first capture of 10 frames instead of 8 in `sites.resolve()` (7ae4a50a, reverted in a966688c): the same site by
   construction (test `node/test/p2-c6-depth.test.js`, deleted with it), identical results in 3+3 fixed runs, but no
   gain: agentsys median 17.56 -> 17.44 s, alakajam 58.79 -> 60.90 s (ranges overlap).

`hazards()` (C4: 16 s -> 50 ms on codex-multi-auth's markers) and the lane bookkeeping do not apply at 1 worker.

## Open: design question D7 (sent to the coordinator 2026-11-28)

The one large per-file cost found (about 19 s of alakajam's 60 s at 1 worker) can only be removed by changing what the
engine records on repositories like alakajam: (a) leave it; (b) make the site lookup independent of the import-time
warm-up (resolveFormatted loads the file's source map itself, or the recorder formats once per source file), then take
the raw stack, with the founder accepting the changed sites and `files_read_outside_tests` as a proof-field change and
tests that the mapped line is the right one; (c) other.
